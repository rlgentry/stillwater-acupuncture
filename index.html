Welcome to Stillwater Acupuncture


Acupuncture is a time-tested practice rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine that involves gently placing very thin, sterile needles at specific points on the body. It is used to support the body’s natural processes and promote overall well-being.

Acupuncture may help relieve pain, ease stress and tension, improve sleep, and support emotional balance. Each treatment is tailored to your individual needs, creating space to slow down, reconnect with yourself, and restore a sense of balance. At Stillwater Acupuncture, we believe whole health begins with caring for the mind and body together. Mind. Body. Whole Health.
