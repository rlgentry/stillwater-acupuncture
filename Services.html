Our services include:

  • Acupuncture
  • Moxibustion
  • Cupping Therapy
  • Tuina Massage

Acupuncture is a time-tested practice rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine that involves gently placing very thin, sterile needles at specific points on the body. It is used to support the body’s natural processes and promote overall well-being.

Moxibustion is a traditional Chinese medicine technique that involves burning dried mugwort (Artemisia argyi) near specific points on the body to stimulate circulation and promote healing.

Cupping Therapy uses suction cups to improve circulation and relieve muscle tension.

Tuina Massage is a therapeutic form of massage that combines acupressure and other techniques to promote healing and relaxation.
